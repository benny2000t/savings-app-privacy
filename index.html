Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 02, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Savings App ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

A. Local Data

Savings App is designed to store your financial goals and transactions locally on your device. This data is not automatically transmitted to our servers.

B. Cloud Sync (Optional)

If you choose to enable Google Cloud Sync, your application data will be stored in your own Google Drive account (within a private "App Data" folder). We do not have access to your Google Drive outside of this specific folder.

C. Advertising Data

We use Google AdMob to serve advertisements. Google may use advertising identifiers (like Android Advertising ID or iOS IDFA) and other information about your device and app usage to serve personalized ads.

  • Privacy Policy for Google Advertising: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the app's functionality.
  • Enable cloud backups and synchronization.
  • Serve relevant advertisements to support the app's development.

3. Data Protection

Your local data is protected by your device's security. Cloud data is protected by Google's security infrastructure.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions, please contact us through the contact information provided in the Google Play Store.
