Frequently Asked Questions

Questions & Answers

Everything you need to know about Savings App.

Is my financial data secure?

Yes. The app saves all records strictly on your local device via SQLite. We never have access to your balances or goals.

Do I need to create an account?

No! You can use the entire app with zero registration. Google login is only needed if you voluntarily enable Google Drive cloud backup.

Does the app work without internet?

Absolutely. Adding goals, logging transactions, and calculating projections work 100% offline.

Where can I read the Privacy Policy?

You can review our full legal terms, data protection details, and account deletion steps on our Privacy Policy page.
